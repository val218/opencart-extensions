What it does

  • Health score from 0 to 100 and a list of what needs attention, most serious first
  • Security: OpenCart and PHP versions, leftover install folder, exposed storage, easy admin names, HTTPS, errors shown to visitors
  • Extensions: missing files, hooks pointing to deleted extensions, every added extension and modification
  • Error log read for you: grouped, explained, traced to the extension causing it
  • SEO and content: missing meta titles, duplicate SEO keywords, missing images, products with price 0
  • Speed and server: PHP extensions, OPcache, database size, old sessions, oversized images
  • EU rules: withdrawal button, lowest price in 30 days, product safety (GPSR), accessibility statement, cookie consent and the download waiver
  • OpenCart 4 readiness for OpenCart 3 shops
  • One-click fixes for safe problems, every fix recorded and undoable; the check itself only reads
