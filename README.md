# OpenCart extensions by val218

Product page: https://val218.github.io/opencart-extensions/

Six extensions for OpenCart 3.0.x and 4.0.x / 4.1.x, sold on the OpenCart Marketplace:

| Extension | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| AI Writer | Product and category descriptions, SEO meta, translations and Journal blog articles with Claude, OpenAI or Gemini | [Guide](docs/AI_WRITER_GUIDE.md) |
| Store Doctor | Health check: security, extensions, errors, SEO, speed and OpenCart 4 readiness, with safe one-click fixes | [Guide](docs/STORE_DOCTOR_GUIDE.md) |
| Real Visitors | Who is on your shop now, sorted into real visitors, customers and bots, with the reason for each | [Guide](docs/REAL_VISITORS_GUIDE.md) |
| Shop Inbox | Shop email inside OpenCart with orders beside each message and follow-up reminders | [Guide](docs/SHOP_INBOX_GUIDE.md) |
| UK Accounting Export | Sales ledger, HMRC expense categories and MTD quarterly figures | [Guide](docs/UK_EXPORT_GUIDE.md) |
| LV Income & Expense Journal | Latvian income and expense journal from paid orders | [Guide](docs/LV_JOURNAL_GUIDE.md) |
