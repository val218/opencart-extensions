What it does

  • Your shop mailbox inside the OpenCart admin - no switching to another app
  • The customer's orders and status shown beside every email
  • Reply with saved templates and your signature; replies keep the email thread together
  • Follow-up reminders: get reminded automatically if the customer does not answer
  • Needs reply, Waiting for customer and Follow-ups due lists, so nothing slips through
  • Newsletters and automatic mail sorted out of the way
  • Works with any mailbox that offers IMAP and SMTP with a password (hosting email, Gmail with an app password, Zoho and most others)
