What it does

  • A question box on every shop page, or product pages only
  • Finds products by name, model, brand, category, tags, attributes and description - typos and word endings included
  • Price questions: "anything under 100?", "cheapest laptop", "over 1000" - in the shopper’s currency, specials included
  • On a product page, "it" means that product: weight, size, colours and options, attributes, stock, price
  • Your own answers for delivery, returns, warranty and the like; information pages are used automatically
  • Off-topic or unknown? It says so honestly and links to your contact page
  • See what people ask and which questions had no answer; write the answer in one click
  • English, Latvian, German, Russian, Spanish and French built in; every word can be changed
  • No API key, no monthly fee, nothing leaves your shop; e-mails and phone numbers are removed from saved questions
