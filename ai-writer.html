What it does

  • Write descriptions and SEO meta for many products at once, or one at a time from the product form
  • Translate your whole catalogue into every store language, keeping HTML and brand names
  • Journal 3 blog articles that link to your real products
  • Your brand voice: describe your shop and tone once, every text follows it
  • Uses only the facts in your product data - told never to invent specs or claims
  • Review everything before it is saved, and undo any change in one click
  • Works with Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI or Google Gemini - you use your own API key and pay the provider directly

How it works

  1. Add your AI provider key in Settings (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini)
  2. Describe your shop and tone once in Brand voice
  3. Pick products, fields and languages, then click Write
  4. Review the results, edit anything, and save - or undo later
