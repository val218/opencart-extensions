A Black Friday hunt

  1. Press New hunt and pick the Black Friday idea: gold coins, find 6, 20% off orders over a minimum, a cap on prizes.
  2. Choose the dates of your sale and save. The coin button appears in your shop on the start date.
  3. Logged-in customers find the coins, 3 a day, each in their own places. Guests see what they can win and a "Log in to play" button.
  4. Winners get a personal code that works once, only on their account, until the date you set.
  5. Results show players, winners, used codes and the sales of the products the coins were found on.

What it does

  • Themes for every season: gold coins, Easter eggs, Christmas gift boxes, Halloween pumpkins, Valentine hearts, or automatic by season
  • Campaigns with dates, a goal, how many hide each day and hints like "A coin is hiding in Shoes"
  • Hide them on the least visited products, any product, products you pick or chosen categories (with Product Funnel: real-visitor counts)
  • The prize is a personal OpenCart coupon: percent off, amount off or free delivery, minimum order, validity, a cap on prizes
  • Cheat-proof: hiding places are chosen by the server for each customer and day, with one-time codes; bots and scripts get nothing
  • Results: players, icons found, winners, used codes and their sales, how far players got, sales of the hunted products before and during the hunt
  • No core files changed, no outside service, no API key, no monthly fee. Works on phones.
